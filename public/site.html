Useful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Can I buy accounts directly from this website?

Yes. Eligible Zerkers, 1 Defence Pures and Med Mains use our BTC/LTC checkout. Discord-only listings and services open our support server instead.

Are calculator prices final?

They are estimates generated from the uploaded pricing spreadsheets. Account condition, gear, supplies and special restrictions can change the final quote.

How will I receive my order?

Account orders and service delivery are coordinated through a private Discord ticket after payment is confirmed.

Can prices be negotiated?

Selected account prices can be discussed through Discord. Service quotes depend on the exact account and method requirements.

Is SalesMan Services affiliated with Jagex?

No. SalesMan Services is an independent third-party marketplace and service provider and is not affiliated with Jagex Ltd.
