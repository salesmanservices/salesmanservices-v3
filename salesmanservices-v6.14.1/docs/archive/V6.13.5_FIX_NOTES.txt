Salesman Services V6.13.5

- Hardened automatic email delivery: paid orders with unsent/failed delivery are retried by the existing Cloudflare scheduled worker.
- A transient Resend/API/worker failure no longer leaves a paid order permanently waiting for manual intervention.
- Preserved the existing admin delivery retry endpoint.
- Recently sold accounts now display in a responsive 3-column grid on desktop, 2 columns on tablet, and 1 column on mobile instead of one long vertical column.
- Bumped checkout asset versions to 6.13.5 for cache busting.
