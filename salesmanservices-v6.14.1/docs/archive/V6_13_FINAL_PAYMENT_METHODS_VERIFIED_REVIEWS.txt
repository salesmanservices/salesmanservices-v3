Salesman Services V6.13 Final

NEW: Dynamic payment methods
- Add, edit, enable, disable, recommend and reorder methods in /admin -> Payment methods.
- BTC and LTC remain automatic blockchain methods.
- New manual methods can show payment details and customer instructions.
- Manual orders reserve the account and wait for admin confirmation.
- Mark a manual order Paid or Completed in /admin -> Orders and Save changes to trigger credential delivery.

IMPROVED: Verified website reviews
- Website feedback from completed orders is marked Verified Purchase.
- Admin can approve/hide, feature, set rating and manage review badges.
- Featured reviews appear first on the homepage.

All V6.12 features, dynamic vouches, timers, vault, checkout and Cloudflare settings are preserved.
